More concretely, we will prove the following milestones:

  • The moduli space of stable curves of genus $g > 1$ is a smooth, proper, irreducible Deligne–Mumford stack of dimension $3g - 3$ which admits a projective coarse moduli space.
  • The moduli space of semistable vector bundles of rank $r$ and degree $d$ over a fixed smooth connected projective curve of genus $g > 1$ is a smooth, universally closed, irreducible algebraic stack of dimension $r^2(g - 1)$ which admits a projective good moduli space.
