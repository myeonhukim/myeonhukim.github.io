Myeonhu Kim

I am a fifth and final-year Ph.D. student in mathematics at Princeton University. My advisors are János Kollár and Jakub Witaszek. I am currently at Northwestern University as a visiting predoctoral fellow.

I work in algebraic geometry and commutative algebra, especially singularities in positive and mixed characteristics. Most of my work so far has been building the theories of numerical invariants in the quasi-$F$ setting. I am also interested in birational geometry and the minimal model program, moduli theory, and non-archimedean analytic geometry.

Research

  1. A Kunz-type theorem for quasi-F-singularities. (In preparation.)
    Abstract

    We establish an analogue and generalization of Kunz’s classical regularity criterion using the Witt Frobenius on truncated Witt rings.

  2. The Witt–Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity and the relative quasi-$F$-rational signature. (In preparation.)
    Abstract

    We develop the theory of $n$-Witt–Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity and relative $n$-quasi-$F$-rational signature, generalizing the classical notions of Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity and relative $F$-rational signature.
